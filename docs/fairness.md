# Comparison contract

The experiment distinguishes a 5,000-update parent, a compressed warm start with
5,000 further updates, budget-limited scratch with 5,000 updates, and two
5,000-plus-5,000 continuation controls. Report the warm parent's training and
compression costs. A positive change relative to the parent does not isolate an
initialisation benefit or demonstrate a benefit caused by compression.

Common assets do not make different published evaluators comparable. This study
uses decoded representation files, the local HKTex renderer, five full-frame
PBR views and RTX 3090 hardware. Published LocalHK albedo measurements, camera
settings and A100 timings are not inserted into the same ranking. A prior
fixed-centre local-kernel pilot lacks the complete moving-centre, density-control
and geodesic-optimiser contracts of LocalHK.

Keep public replication, new Objaverse confirmation, independent-source GSO,
official tuning examples and the historical small cohort separate. Three-seed
panels were fixed before fitting and do not turn three runs of one object into
three independent objects. Means, medians, win counts and bootstrap intervals
are descriptive. Missing pairs and numerical failures are reported explicitly.

The scale and fixed-warmup follow-ups use eight previously observed objects,
one seed, and a uniform fixed-pool objective instead of the main recipe's
priority sampling. The warmup intervention retains each object's physical GPU,
initial parameters, random states and first-batch loss from the reused controls.
Its similar mean endpoint does not establish statistical equivalence or a
general controller advantage. All routes retain the directional diagnostic,
so their instrumented times are not evidence of production acceleration.

Native UV and coded kernel payloads are deployment alternatives. Charge UV PNG,
UV coordinates and texture-face indices; charge the actual codec bitstream,
not its decoded NPZ. Stored representation bytes exclude shared geometry and
the HKTex spectral cache, so they cannot establish total-memory dominance.
Likewise, concurrent experiment timings cannot establish isolated speedups.
A measured nondominated point is a frontier within tested settings, not proof
of global Pareto optimality.

The main image metric is the mean of per-view PSNR. An exactly matching view
can make that mean infinite; retain it and separately report pooled MSE/PSNR
for every method. Pooled PSNR is a supplementary common aggregation and must
not silently replace the registered metric only on a difficult example.
Finite recorded loss is insufficient: all deployed model arrays must be finite
before a model enters the valid comparison table.
